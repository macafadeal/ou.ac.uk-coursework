Data Privacy

We take your data privacy very seriously and you can opt out of all tracking functionality, except for those that are needed to ensure the security of the system and your data.

You can also change your preferences at any time and these will immediately be reflected in how the system works for you and what data the system holds on you.

If you opt out of a tracking feature, some additional functionality may not be available. However, all teaching materials will always be fully available to you, regardless of your settings.
